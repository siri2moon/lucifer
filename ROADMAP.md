# Roadmap

Lucifer Corp started as a toy you watch. The goal is a company you can actually run: angels that take a real client brief and ship a real product, then market it. Here is the road there. Every item is open for contributors, so pick one that excites you.

## 🌌 1. New worlds to work in

The yacht is only the first office. Redemption is a journey, and the office should travel with it.

- **Galaxy station:** the company lifts off and works from a space station drifting through nebulae, with desks on floating platforms and angels flying between them in zero gravity.
- **Heaven's gate:** a marble office among the clouds, unlocked once the company has earned enough good deeds.
- **More worlds:** a mountain monastery, a neon city rooftop, an underwater dome.
- **Theme API:** a theme is a self-contained scene module (environment, desk layout, break-time spots), so anyone can add one without touching the core.
- **Seasons and weather:** day/night cycles, rain and storms that follow the real clock.

## 🤝 2. Angels that work together like a real team

Today work only flows downward. Real teams negotiate.

- **Approvals:** a manager reviews a subordinate's deliverable and approves it or sends it back with comments. Risky actions (spending money, publishing, deploying) wait for your approval.
- **Requests between colleagues:** any angel can ask a peer for help ("Lilith, I need a hero image for this page"). The peer accepts, declines or negotiates a deadline.
- **Review loops:** QA reviews engineering output, the CMO reviews copy, and work goes around until it passes a quality bar.
- **Meetings:** stand-ups and kickoffs where angels gather around a table on the deck, discuss a plan and leave with assignments.
- **Ask instead of guess:** when an angel isn't confident about a decision (a vague brief, two equally good directions, a risky trade-off), it pauses and asks. Its manager is asked first, and if the manager isn't sure either, the question reaches you. The question shows up as a card in the office with a few concrete options to pick from, each with the angel's recommendation and its reasoning, plus a box to type your own answer. The task waits in a "needs input" state, the angel raises a hand at its desk, and work resumes the moment you answer. Your answers are remembered, so the same question isn't asked twice.

## 🎬 3. Real production work

Tools that turn the company into one that delivers real output, not just documents.

- **Video production:** scripts, storyboards and voice-over, rendered with AI video models (Muse AI and others) through a pluggable media-provider interface.
- **Image and brand design:** logos, social visuals and product mockups from image models.
- **Web research:** search and read the web, with sources cited in every deliverable.
- **MCP tools:** connect the company to any MCP server, such as GitHub, Notion, Slack or Google Drive.

## 💻 4. A software studio that ships

Turn a client's request into a running app.

- **Client portal:** clients submit a brief, answer clarifying questions, watch progress and approve milestones.
- **Code in a sandbox:** engineers write, run and test code in an isolated container instead of only writing files.
- **Git workflow:** each feature is a branch and a pull request, reviewed by Michael before merge.
- **One-click deploy** to Cloudflare Pages, Vercel or Fly.io, with a live preview link delivered back to the client.
- **Project templates:** landing page, SaaS starter, mobile app (Expo) and Chrome extension.

## 📈 5. Marketing that brings users

Shipping is half the job. The marketing team makes sure people find what was built.

- **SEO pipeline:** keyword research, technical SEO audits of the shipped site, and blog content clustered around target keywords.
- **Launch kits:** Product Hunt posts, social threads, newsletters and press releases, in every language the product supports.
- **Scheduled publishing** to social channels and blogs (always behind your approval).
- **Analytics loop:** read traffic and conversion data, report what worked and suggest the next round of content.

## ✨ More ideas we'd love help with

- **Redemption meter:** every task delivered honestly adds light. Halos grow brighter, angels level up and new worlds unlock. Failures reported openly also count, because owning a mistake is part of making amends.
- **Budgets and cost dashboard:** a token and money budget per agent, department and project, with alerts before overspending.
- **Company memory:** a shared knowledge base, so angels remember past projects, client preferences and lessons learned.
- **Recurring work:** "every Monday, write the weekly report", run on a schedule.
- **Hire and fire from the UI:** edit the org chart live, give each angel a model, tools and a personality.
- **Company templates marketplace:** share ready-made org charts (agency, game studio, law firm, newsroom) as YAML.
- **Replay mode:** scrub through a finished project's timeline and watch it unfold again in 3D.
- **Voice:** talk to Lucifer, and hear the stand-up read aloud.
- **Multiplayer office:** several humans watching and assigning work in the same company.
